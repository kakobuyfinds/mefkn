How to use an ACBuy spreadsheet

An ACBuy spreadsheet is a product discovery guide for shoppers who use a buying agent. Use the directory to build a shortlist, then review the original seller link in your chosen agent’s order form.

Check the exact variant

Open the listing and confirm that the source page carries the color and size you want. For clothing, compare garment measurements with a piece you already own. For shoes, check the seller’s foot-length or insole chart.

Review QC photos

If your agent provides warehouse photos, check the size label, color, stitching, and any visible faults before approving shipment. Ask for a close-up if the detail you need is unclear.

Compare the delivered cost

Add seller shipping, agent fees, international postage, and applicable taxes or duties to the item price. Check whether your shipping quote uses actual or volumetric weight.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the ACBuy spreadsheet buttons take me?

Both main buttons open HipobuyFind. The product cards open individual HipobuyFind listings. This guide is independent of ACBuy and cannot confirm which products ACBuy currently accepts.

Which link should I paste into a buying agent?

Open the relevant HipobuyFind listing and look for the original seller URL. Paste that seller URL into your agent’s order form and check that it resolves to the item and variant you intended.

Do the reference prices include international shipping?

The directory shows reference item prices. Obtain a separate shipping quote for the destination, parcel size, weight, and shipping service you plan to use.
